# Stokly — Instagram content brief (paste this whole file into a new chat)

You are a senior social-media art director and copywriter. Your job: turn the

## 1. What Stokly is (true facts — never invent others)

Stokly is a restaurant inventory & food-cost SaaS for Azerbaijani HoReCa
(restaurants, cafés, bars). Interface in Azerbaijani and Russian.

What it actually does:
- Recipe costing: live cost per dish/serving, food-cost %, suggested price
- FIFO batch tracking with expiry dates and alerts
- Daily itemized sales that auto-deduct ingredient stock
- Yarımfabrikat (semi-finished preps): produce → stock → sell correctly
- Stock counts with variance reports (theoretical vs actual usage)
- Waste log by reason, with money values
- Owner dashboard: revenue, food cost %, gross profit, purchases, waste,
  inventory value, daily sales trend, top dishes, spend by supplier
- P&L-style finances view per period

Pricing (real): **Sınaq — 0 ₼, 14 gün** (full features, no card) and
**Standart — 99 ₼/ay** (everything, unlimited team). Trial starts via a demo
request on the site.

Audience: restaurant owners/managers in Azerbaijan. Pain points: profit lost in
the kitchen — over-portioning, spoilage, theft, stale supplier prices, no real
food-cost numbers.

IMPORTANT HONESTY RULES: the dashboard screenshots show a **demo restaurant**
("Şəhər Kafe") — treat numbers as illustrative, never as customer results. Do
not fabricate testimonials, customer counts, or savings percentages. Pain-point
claims must stay generic and plausible ("itkilər səssiz böyüyür"), not fake
statistics.

## 2. Brand system — "the kitchen ledger" (use it, don't redesign it)

Concept: the paper artifacts of a restaurant's back office — till receipts,
kitchen tickets, ledger rules, rubber stamps. Editorial, printed, human. The
opposite of glossy gradient SaaS.

Colors (exact):
- Paper (background): `#F4F1E8`  · card paper `#FBFAF5` / receipt white `#FFFEF9`
- Ink (text): `#1C1A14` · secondary `#5B574A` · muted `#8E8A7B`
- Hairlines: `#DDD7C4` (solid) and `#C9C2AB` (dashed)
- Brand teal (accent, use sparingly like a stamp): `#00C896` (legible variant `#00926E`)
- Ledger red (tiny doses: ✗ marks, margin line): `#C2462E`
- Ink slab (dark blocks/final slides): `#16140E` with paper-colored text

Typography (all on Google Fonts):
- Display/headlines: **Bricolage Grotesque** (700/800), tight tracking
- Editorial accent (italic phrases): **Spectral** italic
- Numbers, labels, captions-on-image: **JetBrains Mono** (uppercase, wide letter-spacing)
- Body: DM Sans

Signature motifs (use 1–2 per slide, not all):
- The ✳ asterisk mark (brand logo mark, teal) — bullet, separator, footnote
- Till receipt: dotted leaders (`Satış ......... 2 840 ₼`), dashed rules, barcode, zigzag torn edge
- Rubber stamp: rotated −7°, 2–3px border, mono uppercase, teal (✓ HESABLANDI) or red
- Kitchen ticket: small card with a punch hole, slight tilt
- Section index: `№ 01 — MÖVZU` + thin rule
- Ruled-paper lines + a thin red margin line on the left
- Screenshots framed as "exhibits": thin corner ticks + mono caption `şək. 01 — idarə paneli`

Voice: dry, confident, numbers-forward, zero hype. Azerbaijani first, Russian
second. Short sentences. The receipt does the talking.

## 4. Formats

- Feed/carousel: **1080×1350** (4:5) — primary
- Square grid posts: 1080×1080
- Stories/Reels covers: 1080×1920 (keep text inside the middle 1080×1420 safe area)
- Carousels: 5–7 slides. Slide 1 = hook only (≤8 words). Last slide = CTA.
- Crop screenshots generously — show ONE thing per slide (a KPI row, the chart,
  the receipt), framed as an exhibit on paper, never a full tiny screenshot.
